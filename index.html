Mooney Household Ledger

A private, single-household finance dashboard. Not a commercial product.

What it does

Mooney Household Ledger gives its owner a consolidated, read-only view of the owner's own financial accounts: balances, transactions, investment holdings, and loan details. It connects to financial institutions through Plaid.

The application does not initiate payments, transfers, or any other changes to accounts.

Who uses it

Only the owner and members of the owner's household. The application is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy statement

Effective date: October 12, 2026

  • Data accessed: account names and types, balances, transaction history, investment holdings and transactions, and loan details, retrieved through Plaid with the account holder's consent.
  • How it is used: solely to display budgeting, spending, and net-worth information to the account holder.
  • Storage: data and access credentials are stored privately on systems controlled by the owner, with access limited to the owner. Bank usernames and passwords are never seen or stored by this application; authentication is handled by Plaid and the financial institution.
  • Sharing: data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising, lending, or any purpose other than the one above.
  • Revoking access: connections can be removed at any time through Plaid Portal or through the financial institution's connected-apps settings. Removing a connection stops all further data access.
  • Deletion: data for a removed connection is deleted on request.
